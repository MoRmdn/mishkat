MISHKAT AL-WIRD · 2a DUSK GRID · FULL SCREEN SET
Every screen and state, ready to build
This board is the visual source of truth for the Flutter redesign. Screens are 340×720, grouped by flow, Arabic-first with English (LTR) and dark variants where the layout changes. Colours, type and spacing come from tokens/mishkat.tokens.json, and icons from icons/svg. Athkar text is the repo's placeholder corpus and is not changed.

5.6 Notifications · what the app controls
ANDROID (system layout, our content)
مشكاة · الآن
وقت أذكار الصباح
أصبحنا وأصبح الملك لله · ٦ أذكار
ابدأتأجيل ١٥ د
We control: the small icon (ic_stat_mishkat), the accent tint #4A2A4D, the title, body, action labels, channel name and sound. The OS controls the font, layout, background and button styling.
iOS (system layout, app icon)
وقت أذكار الصباحالآن
أصبحنا وأصبح الملك لله · ٦ أذكار
On iOS the app icon is always shown. We control the title, body, sound and category actions, which appear on long-press. Custom colours and fonts are not possible.
