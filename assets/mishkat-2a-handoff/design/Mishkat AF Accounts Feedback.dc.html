MISHKAT AL-WIRD · 2a DUSK GRID · EXTENSION AF
Accounts & Feedback
Signing in is optional. The app works fully offline and signed out, and nothing is locked behind an account. An account only backs up and syncs progress, favourites and settings across devices. Feedback is short and respectful. Team replies appear under «ملاحظات واقتراحات / Feedback» and are never sent as push notifications. Tokens, type, radii and components are unchanged from Mishkat 2a Screens.
16 · SETTINGS REFACTOR · PROPOSAL
The 5.4 sheet was sized for four controls. With Account, Feedback, the owner Inbox and the legal links it holds about nine, and Account and Feedback push full screens anyway. Opening a page from inside a sheet feels awkward. 16a (recommended) turns Settings into a grouped, scrolling page opened from Home's settings button. 16b keeps the sheet and moves legal into «عن التطبيق».

There is no success colour. "Synced" and "Sent" use a primary check on glowSoft, the same way 3.4 marks a completed session. A green would be the only saturated hue in the product, and these confirmations don't need extra alarm or reward. Destructive actions use the existing error and errorSoft. The Apple and Google buttons use each provider's own colours, which are brand assets, not app tokens. In dark mode each pair maps to its dark twin in mishkat.tokens.json.
