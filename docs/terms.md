## Content

The athkar are transcribed from well-known collections of hadith and the Qur'an,
with their sources shown. The texts and references are being reviewed by
qualified people; errors remain possible, and reports are welcome. The app is an
aid to remembrance and does not replace scholarly guidance.

## Reminders

depends on permissions, battery settings and the manufacturer's handling of
background apps. Delivery of every reminder, or at the exact minute, is not
guaranteed. Prayer times are calculated astronomically and may differ slightly
from a local mosque.
